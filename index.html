    Everyday habits

    Small habits add up

    Sleep consistently.

    A regular sleep schedule supports concentration, mood and recovery.

    Drink more water.

    Keep water nearby and choose it often, especially during hot weather.

    Protect your skin.

    Use shade, clothing and sunscreen when spending time in strong sunlight.

    Eat a variety of foods.

    Mix vegetables, fruit, protein foods and starchy staples across your meals.

    Move regularly.

    Break up long periods of sitting with safe, comfortable movement.

    Take care of your mind.

    Make time for family, friends, hobbies, relaxation and asking for support when needed.
